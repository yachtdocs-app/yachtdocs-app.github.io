YachtDocs and Captain's Chest

Android apps for yacht owners and skippers: keep the boat's and crew's documents in one place and fill official forms in minutes.

What the apps do

  • Fill and send official forms: Israel entry report, IMO FAL 5 crew list, GMDSS certificate update, Larnaca and other marina berth requests.
  • Keep crew, passenger and vessel documents in an encrypted, PIN-protected Vault on the phone.
  • Reminders before documents expire.
  • Captain's Chest adds a logbook, expenses and a Locker for things stored on board.
  • Optional sync between phones through the boat's own Google Drive.

Your data

Your data stays on your phone. If you turn on sync, it is also kept in a folder of your own Google Drive, which only the app can use. The developer has no server and never receives your data. Read the full Privacy Policy.
