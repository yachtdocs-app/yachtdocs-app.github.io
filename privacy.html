Privacy Policy

YachtDocs and Captain's Chest · Last updated: 16.10.2026 · Developed and maintained by Eugene Dorfman

This policy explains how the YachtDocs and Captain's Chest apps for Android ("the app") handle your information. In short: your data stays on your phone and, only if you turn on sync, in your own Google Drive. The developer has no server and never receives your data.

1. Data collection

The developer does not run any server and does not collect, receive or have access to your personal data, documents or files. The app has no analytics, advertising or tracking.

2. Data you store in the app

You may store crew and passenger identity details (names, dates of birth, passport and ID numbers), vessel documents, voyage details (ports, dates, flag state), logbook and expense records, and signatures used on forms. This data is stored on your device. Documents in the Vault are encrypted and protected by your PIN. The developer cannot access or recover this data.

3. Google Drive sync (optional)

If you choose to turn on sync, you sign in with a Google account of your choice. The app then keeps your app data identical on all phones signed in with that same account by storing it in a folder in that account's Google Drive.

  • The app requests only the drive.file permission. It can see and change only the files and folder that the app itself created in your Drive. It cannot see any of your other Drive files, your email, contacts or anything else.
  • The app uses your Google account email address only to show which account is connected and to make sure all phones use the same account.
  • Vault documents are uploaded in their encrypted form; they cannot be read without your Vault PIN.
  • The data goes directly from your phone to your Google Drive. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Google's own Privacy Policy applies to data stored in Google Drive.
  • You can stop sync at any time with "Sign out" in the app's Settings, remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and delete the sync folder from your Google Drive.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Sending forms by email or messaging

When you send a form, the app opens your own email or messaging app with the file attached. The app does not send messages itself and does not access your email account.

5. Third-party services

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. The only external services it uses are Google Sign-In and the Google Drive API, and only when you turn on sync.

6. Backups

You can create a backup file and store or transfer it yourself. The developer has no access to it.

7. Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 16.

8. Changes to this policy

Updates are posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
